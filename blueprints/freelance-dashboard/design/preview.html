Mirroring

ElementMirrors?Reason
Navigation arrow, next and previousYesExpresses movement through the interface
Linear progress bar, breadcrumb, paginationYesFills along the reading direction

Media transport controlsNoExpresses media direction, not reading direction
Checkmark, keyboard, cameraNoObjects and non-directional glyphs
